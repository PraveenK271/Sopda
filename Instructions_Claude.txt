# ERP Software Development Instructions

## Project Name

Gemini ERP

## Objective

Build a Windows-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) application similar to Tally ERP, specifically designed for Indian trading and distribution businesses.

The software should be optimized for:

* Inventory Management
* GST Billing
* Accounting
* Purchase Management
* Sales Management
* OCR-Based Invoice Processing
* AI-Powered Business Intelligence

The application must run locally on Windows systems and support future migration to multi-user environments.
